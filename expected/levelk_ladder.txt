Reading:
 - The RM(n-1-k,n)* code identity holds (moment-delta = true delta_k), and the slack vs
   delta_k grows with level: 1 (k=3), 7-15 (k=4), 21 (k=5), 51 (k=6). Radical is O(n^k),
   cheap, no decode -> the floor 2(n-d_k) is the higher-level analogue you actually compute.
 - CAVEAT on the nullity columns: nu here is for the min-weight-RESIDUE lift (canonical_phi),
   NOT the natural gate (-1)^f.  For the natural gate the identity nu=n-d_k is a k=3-only
   phenomenon; at k>=4 nu can strictly exceed n-d_k (counterexample e_4 on n=5: nu=5>4).
   See levelk_nullity_study.py -- there A<=R and nu>=n-d_k hold unconditionally and the
   floor 2(n-d_k) still beats nu (100%), but it is NOT equal to 2*nu for k>=4.
 - Level 2 (mod-4 pure quadratic) is the Clifford regime, nu=0 -> a vacuous comparison.
   The real 'quadratic does not beat nullity' is the mixed-degree -2r penalty: see
   experiment_mixed_nullity.py (CS-type quadratic mass embedded at level 3).
